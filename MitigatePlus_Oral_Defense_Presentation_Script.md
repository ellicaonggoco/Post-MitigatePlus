## 💡 DEFENSE Q&A QUICK-REFERENCE CHEAT SHEET
* **Q1: "Bakit post-disaster mitigation lang? Bakit walang evacuation center tracking?"**  
  * *Answer:* *"As per Prof. Abad's recommendation in Capstone 1 and Section 1.3 of our manuscript, we delimited our scope exclusively to the post-disaster phase. Evacuation modeling is already handled by city sirens and barangay marshals, whereas the primary operational breakdown identified by Manila DRRMD occurs during relief distribution, damage triage, and beneficiary duplication."*
* **Q2: "Paano ninyo napipigilan ang double claims sa field?"**  
  * *Answer:* *"At the database level, we enforce a MongoDB Compound Unique Index on `{ distributionEventId: 1, householdId: 1 }`. If a beneficiary's QR is scanned twice, the database rejects the write, and Socket.io instantly flashes an audio-visual red alert on both the staff scanner and the admin dashboard fraud stream."*
* **Q3: "Bakit headcount-scaled ang relief at hindi standard 1 pack?"**  
  * *Answer:* *"Traditional 1-pack policies cause severe under-allocation for large households and food waste for small households. MitigatePlus implements Algorithm 2 (Page 29 of manuscript): 1 to 4 members receive 1 base pack, 5 to 8 receive 2 base packs, and 9+ receive 3 base packs, supplemented with demographic-specific add-ons for infants and seniors."*
