Project Context - Urban Disaster Vulnerability

Speaker 1
VISUAL SLIDE CUE: Manila disaster vulnerability, RA 10121, frontline barangay challenges.
SPOKEN DEFENSE SCRIPT:

"The City of Manila is one of the most disaster-prone urban centers in the country, facing an average of 20 tropical cyclones annually. Heavy monsoon rains consistently inundate low-lying barangays and informal settlements along waterways, as highlighted by UNDRR and the World Bank.

Under Republic Act No. 10121, frontline disaster response is legally mandated to local barangay councils. However, traditional manual, paper-based relief operations break down during flooding, resulting in chaotic queues, missing beneficiary records, and critical delivery delays."

Panel Q&A Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet
Q1: "Bakit post-disaster mitigation lang? Bakit walang evacuation center tracking?"
"As per Prof. Abad's recommendation in Capstone 1 and Section 1.3 of our manuscript, we delimited our scope exclusively to the post-disaster phase. Evacuation modeling is already handled by city sirens and barangay marshals, whereas the primary operational breakdown identified by Manila DRRMD occurs during relief distribution, damage triage, and beneficiary duplication."
Q2: "Paano ninyo napipigilan ang double claims sa field?"
"At the database level, we enforce a MongoDB Compound Unique Index on `{ distributionEventId: 1, householdId: 1 }`. If a beneficiary's QR is scanned twice, the database rejects the write, and Socket.io instantly flashes an audio-visual red alert on both the staff scanner and the admin dashboard fraud stream."
Q3: "Bakit headcount-scaled ang relief at hindi standard 1 pack?"
"Traditional 1-pack policies cause severe under-allocation for large households and food waste for small households. MitigatePlus implements Algorithm 2 (Page 29 of manuscript): 1 to 4 members receive 1 base pack, 5 to 8 receive 2 base packs, and 9+ receive 3 base packs, supplemented with demographic-specific add-ons for infants and seniors."
