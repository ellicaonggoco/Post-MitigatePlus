1. Executive System Overview

MitigatePlus is an automated, data-driven, and fraud-resistant disaster relief management platform developed to streamline post-disaster response across Manila's 897 barangays. It eliminates ghost beneficiaries, automates quota allocation based on real-time household demographics, prevents duplicate relief claims through compound unique database indexing, and ensures 100% Commission on Audit (COA) compliance.

System Architecture Composition:
  • Web Admin Operations Center (Vercel): React 18, Vite, Lucide Icons, Leaflet GIS, Framer Motion.
  • Mobile Application (Expo / React Native): Dual-portal for Citizen Beneficiaries & Field Staff Scanners.
  • Backend REST API & WebSocket (Render): Node.js, Express, MongoDB Atlas, Socket.IO, Semaphore SMS Gateway, Cloudinary CDN.

3. Web Admin Modules: Click-by-Click Guide & Technical Actions

1. Dashboard (/) Command Center

Purpose: Live KPI monitoring of city-wide disaster response, beneficiary reach, and critical alerts.

  • When clicking View Queue: Navigates directly to the Verification Queue to inspect pending citizen ID registrations.
  • When clicking Schedule Event: Opens the Distribution Events creator to launch a new relief drive.
  • When clicking Inspect Warehouse: Navigates to Warehouse Inventory to check available food packs and water stock.
2. Verification Queue (/verification-queue) Gatekeeper

Purpose: Fraud barrier where Barangay Officials review uploaded Government IDs before QR passes are activated.

  • When clicking Approve: Opens confirmation dialog. Upon approval, backend marks household as verified, activates the cryptographic QR Code, and sends an automated SMS notification to the resident.
  • When clicking Reject: Prompts the official for a rejection reason (e.g. Blurry ID / Non-resident) and notifies the citizen via SMS to re-upload.
3. Smart Priority Index (/priority-index) Algorithmic Telemetry

Purpose: Algorithmic vulnerability ranking scoring all 897 barangays from 0 to 100 based on damage severity and vulnerable demographics.

  • When clicking Deploy Relief for Barangay [X]: Automatically routes to Distribution Events, pre-fills the target barangay, auto-populates the verified household census count, and opens the creation form.
4. Barangay GIS Risk Heatmap (/heatmap) Spatial GIS

Purpose: Vector choropleth map rendering all 897 Manila barangay polygon boundaries color-coded by real-time risk.

  • Single-Click on any Polygon: Opens the Barangay Inspector Drawer showing verified families, damage breakdown, and past distributions.
  • When clicking Deploy Relief in Drawer: Direct 1-click route to schedule distribution for that specific barangay.
5. Pre-Event Relief Demand & Quota Assessment (/special-request-relief) 100% Automated Quota Engine

Purpose: Fully automated pre-event assessment calculating exact relief quotas without relying on mobile citizen requests.

  • Automated Formula Breakdown:
    • 🍱 Family Food Packs: 1-4 members = 1 pack; 5-8 members = 2 packs; 9+ members = 3 packs.
    • 💧 Potable Water: 1 container (10L Jug) per verified household.
    • 👵 Senior Care Kits: Matched with registered 60+ seniors in the barangay.
    • 🍼 Infant Milk Packs: Matched with registered 0-2 yo infants.
    • 🏠 Shelter Repair Kits: Matched with verified Totally Damaged homes.
  • Warehouse Feasibility Pre-Check: Validates warehouse stocks and confirms 100% Sufficient prior to truck mobilization.
  • When clicking Schedule Event with Computed Quotas: Transfers all calculated quotas directly into the event creation modal.
6. Distribution Events (/distribution-events) Operations & Auto-Dispatch

Purpose: Scheduling relief drives, auto-dispatching warehouse inventory, and live ground monitoring.

  • When creating an Event: Selecting a Barangay auto-detects verified households and displays live assessment quotas. Submitting the event automatically decrements warehouse stock and logs an official dispatch audit entry.
  • When clicking Broadcast Announcement: Instantly pushes a localized relief schedule notification to citizen mobile devices in that barangay.
  • When clicking Close Event: Marks drive as completed, closes QR scanning, and finalizes records for audit.
7. Relief Warehouse Inventory (/warehouse-inventory) COA Logistics Tracking

Purpose: Central storage inventory tracking, shipment replenishment, and emergency off-event dispatch logging.

  • When clicking + Receive: Records incoming deliveries from DSWD/Donors with Supplier, PO #, Waybill #, and Receiving Officer. Increases stock.
  • When clicking - Dispatch: Logs authorized off-event dispatches (Inter-agency transfer to Red Cross/Hospital, Evacuation Center Augmentation, or Damaged Goods write-off) with Purpose, Destination, Approving Official, Vehicle Plate #, and Reference DR #.
  • Stock Movement & Audit Trail Table: Permanent immutable log of every stock movement for Commission on Audit transparency.
8. Fraud Interception Stream (/fraud-interception) Cybersecurity & Anti-Fraud

Purpose: Live cybersecurity stream capturing duplicate QR claim attempts blocked by database compound indexes.

  • Displays offender household details, QR ID, attempted distribution event, timestamp, and field staff scanner ID.
9. Announcements Board (/announcements) Public Broadcast

Purpose: Official broadcast system to push advisories and schedules with smart action triggers to citizen mobile apps.

  • Supports City-Wide or Specific Barangay Targeting. Mobile users receive actionable buttons (e.g. "View Schedule", "Report Damage").
10. Reports & COA Audit (/reports) Official Liquidation

Purpose: 1-Click generation of official Commission on Audit (COA) Circular 2014-002 compliant liquidation masterlists.

  • When clicking Export COA Liquidation Masterlist (CSV): Generates a full masterlist with Claim Receipt #, Beneficiary Name, Government ID #, Family Size, Exact Packs Received, Disbursing Officers, and Timestamps.

4. Mobile Application: Resident & Staff Workflows

Resident / Citizen Flow

  1. Registration & SMS OTP: Resident enters household head info, family members, and verifies phone number via SMS OTP. Uploads Government ID.
  2. Cryptographic Digital QR Pass: Once approved by Barangay Official, the QR pass unlocks on the home screen with family size and entitlement. Works 100% offline via encrypted local cache.
  3. Report Damage: Citizen selects damage severity, captures photo using phone camera, and device tags exact GPS coordinates (uploaded to Cloudinary CDN).
  4. Claims History & Alerts: Tracks every relief claim received and views real-time official announcements from the City Mayor and Barangay Captain.

Field Staff Scanner Flow

  1. Login & Select Active Drive: Staff authenticates with employee credentials and selects the active distribution drive.
  2. Camera QR Scan: Staff scans the resident's QR code. App displays exact entitlement (e.g. 2 Food Packs, 1 Water Jug, 1 Senior Kit).
  3. Anti-Duplicate Fraud Protection: If the QR pass was already claimed in the event, the app immediately flashes a RED DUPLICATE WARNING and blocks the claim.
  4. Confirm Release: Staff releases goods, taps Confirm Release, and the record syncs to the server with staff ID and timestamp.

5. The 7-Step End-to-End Operational Lifecycle

Step 1: Citizen Registration & Verification — Citizen registers on Mobile App with SMS OTP & ID -> Barangay Official inspects and approves in Verification Queue -> QR Pass turns ACTIVE.
Step 2: Disaster Telemetry — Citizens submit Damage Reports with GPS & Photo on Mobile -> Web Admin Heatmap & Priority Index automatically rank affected barangays.
Step 3: Pre-Event Assessment — LGU Admin inspects target Barangay -> System auto-computes Food Packs, Water, Senior & Infant Kits -> Validates warehouse stock feasibility.
Step 4: Event Creation & Auto-Dispatch — Admin creates Distribution Event -> Warehouse inventory is automatically deducted -> Announcement is broadcasted to citizen mobile apps.
Step 5: Ground Distribution & Anti-Fraud Scan — Field Staff scans citizen QR Pass on Mobile -> System validates entitlement & blocks duplicate attempts -> Goods released.
Step 6: COA Liquidation & Recovery — Admin exports 1-click COA Liquidation Masterlist -> Beneficiaries are monitored in Recovery Tracker until fully restored.

6. Technical & Security Highlights for Faculty Panel

  • Zero Duplicate Claims: Compound Unique MongoDB Index { distributionEventId: 1, householdId: 1 } prevents double-dipping at the database engine level.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Strict middleware hierarchy (lgu_superadmin > lgu_admin > barangay_official > field_staff > resident).
  • Offline Resilience: Digital QR passes and offline claim queues cache securely on mobile devices and synchronize automatically when connection resumes.
  • COA Compliance: Every transaction creates an immutable, timestamped AuditLog and WarehouseLog.
