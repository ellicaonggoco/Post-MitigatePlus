Executive Summary: Why MitigatePlus is Groundbreaking

Existing government disaster management platforms (such as DSWD DROMIC and local LGU relief portals) rely on macro-level aggregate summaries, first-come first-served queueing, and flat-rate food packages regardless of family headcount. MitigatePlus introduces four mathematically grounded, client-auditable innovations that transform emergency relief from an arbitrary political distribution into a transparent, right-sized, and accountable pipeline.

1. Foundational Security: Anti-Duplicate-Claim Interception System
Security Core
✗ Traditional LGU & DSWD Flaw
  • Double-Dipping & Ghost Claimants: Individuals queue multiple times across different distribution points or barangays using fake IDs.
  • Paper-based logs: Hand-written signatures cannot be validated in real-time across concurrent distribution stations.
✓ MitigatePlus Innovation
  • Dynamic Cryptographic QR Tokens: Each verified household is assigned a single-use, digitally signed QR pass tied to MongoDB Atlas.
  • Millisecond Cross-Station Lock: Once scanned, status flips to claimed; secondary scan attempts trigger real-time blocked fraud alerts.
Panel Spoken Defense Line (Taglish):
"Sir/Ma'am, ang Foundational Security ng MitigatePlus ay ang Anti-Duplicate-Claim Interception System. Sa tradisyonal na pamamahagi, laganap ang 'double-dipping' o pagpila nang paulit-ulit sa iba't ibang barangay. Sa MitigatePlus, bawat pamilya ay may cryptographic QR token na instant-invalidated sa cloud sa sandaling ma-scan ng field officer, kaya imposible ang multiple claims."

2. Core Novelty 2: Smart Recovery Priority Index (RPI) Engine (Algorithm 1)
Algorithm 1 (Manuscript Sec 3.2 L343-356)
✗ Traditional LGU & DSWD Flaw
  • First-Come, First-Served / Palakasan: Relief is given to whoever lines up first or knows officials, leaving bedridden seniors and totally damaged homes unserved.
  • Macro Only: DSWD DROMIC shows only city totals without household ranking.
✓ MitigatePlus Innovation
  • Objective Scoring (0 to 100): Ranks all families mathematically without human bias or political favoritism.
  • Prioritization Tiers: Tier 1 High (≥75 pts), Tier 2 Medium (≥50 pts), Tier 3 Low (<50 pts) for prioritized dispatching.
Algorithm 1: RPI Mathematical Formulation
RPI = (Damage Points × 0.40) + (Vulnerability Points × 0.40) + (Waiting Factor × 0.20)
• Damage Points: Total Destruction = 100 | Severe = 75 | Moderate = 50 | Minor = 25
• Vulnerability Points: (Senior × 20) + (PWD × 25) + (Infant × 20) + (Pregnant × 15), capped at 100
• Waiting Factor: min(Days Pending × 10, 100)

"Sa halip na first-come, first-served o palakasan sa barangay, binuo namin ang Algorithm 1: Smart Recovery Priority Index. Awtomatiko nitong kinakalkula ang 0 hanggang 100 score batay sa 40% Structural Damage, 40% Demographic Vulnerabilities tulad ng seniors at PWDs, at 20% Waiting Time upang matiyak na ang pinaka-nangangailangan ang unang maabutan ng ayuda."

3. Core Novelty 3: Right-Sized Relief Allocation Engine (Algorithm 2)
Algorithm 2 (Manuscript Sec 3.2 L357-366)
✗ Traditional LGU & DSWD Flaw
  • Flat 1-Box Rule: Ang pamilyang may 1 miyembro ay tumatanggap ng 1 kahon, at ang pamilyang may 8 hanggang 10 miyembro ay 1 kahon din!
  • Malnutrition & Wastage: Nagdudulot ng gutom sa malalaking pamilya habang labis sa maliliit na kabahayan.
✓ MitigatePlus Innovation
  • Dynamic Scaled Entitlement: Awtomatikong kinakalkula sa scanner kung ilang base packs at dagdag na kilo ng bigas at de-lata ang ibibigay.
  • Demographic Nutrition Add-ons: Awtomatikong may +1 Senior Pack at +1 Infant Care Pack para sa mga may sanggol o lolo/lola.
Algorithm 2: Right-Sizing Mathematical Formulation
Base Food Packs = ⌈ Headcount / 5 ⌉
Extra Rice (kg) = Max(0, Headcount - 5) × 2.0 kg
Extra Canned Goods = Max(0, Headcount - 5) × 3.0 cans
• Demographic Add-Ons: +1 Senior Nutrition Pack (if age ≥ 60) | +1 Infant Care Kit (if age 0-2 yo)
Panel Spoken Defense Line (Taglish):
"Ang aming ikatlong novelty ay ang Algorithm 2: Right-Sized Relief Allocation Engine. Binasag namin ang lumang 'Flat 1-Box Rule' kung saan parehong 1 kahon lang ang natatanggap ng 2-member at 10-member family. Sa MitigatePlus, kapag lumagpas sa 5 members, awtomatikong nagko-compute ang scanner ng base food packs plus 2 kg extra rice at 3 extra canned goods bawat dagdag na tao, kasama ang nakalaang maintenance packs para sa seniors at sanggol."

4. Core Novelty 4: Assistance Gap Detection & 5-Stage Recovery Tracker
Post-Distribution Telemetry
✗ Traditional LGU & DSWD Flaw
  • One-and-Done Distribution: Pagkatapos mamahagi sa covered court, tapos na ang tungkulin ng LGU. Walang tracking kung nakabangon ba ang pamilya.
  • Zero Longitudinal Data: Walang visual dashboard na nagpapakita kung sino pa ang walang trabaho o wasak pa ang bubong.
✓ MitigatePlus Innovation
  • Longitudinal 5-Stage Tracking: Sinusubaybayan ang bawat kabahayan mula Stage 1 hanggang Stage 5: Displaced → Temporary Shelter → Repairing Home → Livelihood Transition → Fully Recovered.
  • Integrated Cash-for-Work & Rehabilitation: Direktang nagtutugma sa mga nawalan ng hanapbuhay patungo sa barangay cleanup at rehabilitation jobs.
Panel Spoken Defense Line (Taglish):
"Hindi natatapos ang MitigatePlus sa pamamahagi ng de-lata. Ang ikaapat naming novelty ay ang 5-Stage Longitudinal Recovery Tracker. Sinusubaybayan ng admin dashboard ang telemetry ng bawat pamilya mula sa pagiging 'Displaced' hanggang sa maideklara silang 'Fully Recovered' sa tulong ng shelter kits at Cash-for-Work livelihood coordination."
